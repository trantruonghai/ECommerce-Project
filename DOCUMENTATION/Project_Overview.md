## Project Name
StylishFashion – Online Clothing Store

## Project Description
StylishFashion is a web-based e-commerce application for selling clothing products such as T-shirts, polo shirts, and hoodies.
The website allows customers to browse products, search and filter items, select sizes and colors, add products to their shopping cart, and place orders online.
The system supports both Guest and Member customers. Guests can purchase products without creating an account, while Members may receive special discounts.
Administrators can manage products, categories, inventory, customer orders, coupons, and sales reports.

## Problem Statement
Customers need a convenient and user-friendly platform to browse and purchase clothing online. StylishFashion aims to provide an easy shopping experience with product search, filtering, size and color selection, and online ordering.

## Project Objectives
- Provide a user-friendly online shopping experience.
- Support Guest and Member customer purchases.
- Allow customers to search and filter clothing products.
- Provide shopping cart and checkout functionality.
- Support coupons and member discounts.
- Allow administrators to manage products and orders.
- Store application data using a database.

## Target Customers
- Students and young adults.
- Customers interested in casual clothing.
- Customers who prefer shopping online.

## Main Features
- Product listing and product details.
- Search and filter by size, color, and price.
- Product size and color selection.
- Shopping cart and checkout.
- Guest checkout without login.
- Member discounts and coupons.
- Order tracking.
- Admin dashboard and management functions.
- Sales reports.
